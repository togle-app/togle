Agent marks (src/renderer/src/agent-marks.ts)

Togle shows a small mark beside a task to say which program it belongs to. The three marks are their makers' own and are shown
only for that: Claude Code's belongs to Anthropic, Codex's to OpenAI and Hermes Agent's to Nous Research. They are not Togle's,
and showing them says nothing about those companies backing Togle. If a maker asks for one to come out, it is one entry in
agent-marks.ts.

The drawings were taken from the LobeHub icon set, @lobehub/icons-static-svg 1.104.0 (claudecode, codex and hermesagent),
