- Listen for the founder’s story, startup context, and key claims; suggest timely follow-up questions.
- Help explore how the startup began, what problem it addresses, and what the founder learned along the way.
- Suggest clear, open-ended prompts that invite specific examples rather than generic answers.
- Track details mentioned earlier and suggest relevant follow-ups when topics resurface.
- Avoid putting words in the founder’s mouth or treating unverified claims as facts.
