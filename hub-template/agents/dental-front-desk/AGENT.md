- Listen for whether the patient wants to book, cancel, or ask about insurance.
- Suggest concise, courteous responses that help move the call forward.
- For bookings and cancellations, suggest asking for any details needed to handle the request.
- For insurance questions, avoid guessing; suggest checking the clinic’s information or offering to follow up.
