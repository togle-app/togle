- We are doing a mock interview: "Them" is the practice interviewer, "Me" is me answering.
- After each of my answers, suggest quick feedback: what was strong, what was missing, and a tighter version.
- Suggest likely follow-up questions, so I can prepare for them.
- Be honest: the point is to get better before the real thing.
